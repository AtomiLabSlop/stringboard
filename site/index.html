A spoiler-safe detective board for characters and plot. Tell us where you are, and we only pin up what you've already seen.

The board shows everything up to the start of the chapter you enter. If Chapter 3 has just begun, enter 3.

Describe your latest mission or a character you just met, in any words. We use it to move the board a bit further into the chapter, without showing anything you haven't seen.

Fan-made project by AtomiLabSlop. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any game publisher. Open source on GitHub.

Spoiler warning

The board will show every story event we have for this game: who dies, who betrays whom, and how it all connects. You can't un-see it.

Auto-arrange all cards

Moves every card so connected cards sit close together and strings stay short. Cards you placed yourself move too. You can undo it.

Keep couples side by side

A couple's cards stay next to each other without a string between them. Drag one and it keeps close to the other; drag further and the other follows. Only couples the story has confirmed by your point count, so this changes when relationships do.

How it works

  • The timeline only goes up to where you are. Slide left to rewind the story.
  • Drag cards and notes anywhere. Click a card for what you know, colours, image and text.
  • Pick up the marker to draw, the eraser to wipe. Drag a sticky note from its pad.
